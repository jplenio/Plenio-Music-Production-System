## Unreleased

**Briefs that show their template, your own lyrics, freer covers and bolder creativity.** Choosing a style template fills the brief's fields visibly, every field is a list of the templates' values with your own value always possible, and a sung song takes a lyrics idea or your own lyrics, sung as written. The templates say how a song sounds in their description and what it is about in a lyrics idea of its own. A cover's *original notes* slider leaves out the source's notes step by step, so YuE2 interprets more freely. Creativity goes to 200: its default is now 100, the experimental setting that gave the best songs, and 101-200 goes further. Titles name what a song is about, taken from its lyrics.

### Added

- **Your own lyrics**: a sung Song Brief (and one with sparse vocals) has the choice **lyrics** - *written from the idea below* (default) or *my own, sung as written below* - and the field **lyrics idea or text**. With your own lyrics the writer writes only the title, the style and the cover-art prompt; the words stay exactly as written (their section tags kept; without tags every block of lines becomes a verse and a block that comes back a chorus), and the title comes from them. In the Plenio Player's *Make a song* form too.
- **Original notes** on the Cover Brief (0-100, 100 by default): how many of the source's notes the score keeps. Lower values leave out notes of the instrument line and the chords step by step, down to about a quarter at 0 - the notes on strong beats and each section's first chord stay, and nothing new is written. A sung cover's melody keeps every note, so its words stay sung (measured: 78 % of the words heard at 0, 83 % at 100; with the melody thinned under 20 %); an instrumental cover's melody is thinned too, keeping its phrase starts. The summary says how many were kept; the player and the song page show the value.
- **Lists in the briefs**: genre, mood, tempo, key, meter, language, voice, theme and lead instrument offer every value the style templates have (and common keys, meters, languages and tempos); **✎ type your own …** takes any text, and a value saved in a workflow shows even when no template has it.
- **11 new style templates** for occasions and genres that had none: a birthday song (English and German), party Schlager, a German carnival song, a wedding first dance, a German Liedermacher song, pop rock, melodic trap, Latin pop, J-rock and Italian pop - 250 templates in all.
- **Creativity up to 200**: 101-150 stays *experimental* with freer sampling, 151-200 is *adventurous* - a fresh blend with a clear touch of another genre, a signature instrument or sound, a surprising perspective in the lyrics; two unexpected colours in a YuE2 style, two surprising turns in a MiniMax caption. YuE2's sampling rises to the edge of what is reported to stay coherent (at 200: plan 1.0 / 0.96 / 64, take 1.15 / 0.98 / 200).

### Changed

- **A template fills the fields you see**: choosing a style template writes its values into the brief - description, genre, mood, tempo, key, meter, language, voice, theme, the lyrics idea, the lead instrument - and sets its vocals, length and melody. Another template replaces what a template put there and leaves what you changed; an empty field gets the template's value again. The shipped workflows show their template's values, and a workflow saved before fills its empty fields when it opens. The panel says which fields hold the template's values and which yours; *Refill from template* and *Use template choices* replace *Copy template text* and *Reset all to template*. The Song API and the player write the same values into the workflow kept with a song.
- **The templates' description and lyrics idea apart**: the description says how a song sounds - character, instruments, arrangement -, the new field `lyrics` what its words are about. Every sung template moved its lyrics sentences there, so a brief's description no longer mixes the two.
- **Creativity's default is 100**: new briefs write and sample as 1.4 did at its experimental 100. Every value up to 100 means what it meant before (50 the reference), so saved workflows and finished songs keep theirs - a 1.4 workflow at 50 stays at 50. The player's filter row and search know the new range (`creativity:>100`, `creativity:adventurous`).
- **Titles name what a song is about, not how it sounds**: the writer takes the title from the lyrics - or from the theme and description when there are none -, and a proposed title made of genre, mood, tempo or instrument words (*Midnight Groove*, *Smooth Jazz Night*) is left out while another is offered, unless the words are sung in the song.
- **Older workflows and API prompts keep working**: a Song Brief saved before opens with the lyrics choice set to the template's idea, and an API prompt made with 1.4 runs unchanged - the new inputs are optional.

## 1.4.1 - 2026-10-30

**Plenio Nova calm and sharp, any visual with one click, songs filtered by their stars, and a quicker export.** Plenio Nova's visuals no longer flicker at their edges and in their finest detail - most of all the Mandelbulb, Spectrum City and the Mandala -, their stars shine steadily, no strip of another picture shows at a picture's edge, and *Balanced* draws them at the screen's own size. In the visuals' settings a visual's name shows it at once, and *Fixed* lists every Nova visual, those this graphics card does not play smoothly greyed with why. The player shows a song's lyrics as sung only when you choose it, and filters the songs by your stars and by the measured ones. Export Release hears the lyrics on the song by default - the vocal stem is a choice that takes extra time - and says how long each of its steps took. A section the writer filled with what the band plays is no longer sung.

### Added

- **Any visual at once**: in the visuals' settings (✦ in Now playing, or **V**), a click on a visual's name shows it right away - with *Fixed* it stays, otherwise the mode takes over again at the next change; its tick lets the mode choose it, as before.
- **Songs filtered by their stars**: *My stars* and *Measured stars* among the library's filters - 5, 4-4½, 3-3½, 2-2½, below 2, or not rated / not measured yet; typed as `stars:>=4`, `measured:<2` or `stars:none`. The running player measures the songs exported before 1.4.0 in the background, one at a time and resting as long as each took, so their measured stars come in by and by.
- **How long each step of an export took**: Export Release's summary and ComfyUI's log say it - the audio files, the vocal stem, the lyrics ASR (and how much of it its worker spent hearing), the lyrics' timing, the quality, the record and page - so a slow export shows where the time went.

### Changed

- **The lyrics as written, unless you choose as sung**: the player no longer shows a song's lyrics *as sung* by itself (it did where the take departed far from its lyrics) - the lyrics ASR does not always hear the word sung, a rare word or a name. *As sung* is one click away, on the song's page, in the player bar or with **W**, and kept for that song; *Settings › Lyrics* still sets it for every song. The rule *Auto* chosen before counts as *As written*.
- **Plenio Nova at the screen's own size**: *Plenio Drop and Nova › Quality › Balanced*, the default, draws Nova's visuals at the full size of the screen (it drew three quarters of it and scaled them up), and *Sharp* finer still - up to one and a half times each way - smoothed down onto the screen; each visual as far as the graphics card keeps it smooth. Plenio Drop draws as before.
- **Steady stars**: the stars of the nebula, the black hole, the galaxy, the northern lights, the moonlit sea, Spectrum City and Outrun no longer blink: each is a soft dot whose light stays the same as it moves between pixels, and a star smaller than the screen can show fades out instead of sparkling. The galaxy's arms brighten with the mids, where its clusters sparkled with the highs.
- Spectrum City flies a little slower, and the Mandelbulb's shape breathes with the music's level rather than jumping with every bass note.
- **Export Release hears the lyrics on the song by default**: *lyrics heard on* is *the song* in new workflows and the templates, and shows among the node's options (it was an advanced one). *vocal stem* times the words a little more exactly, but its separation takes about half a minute per take on a free graphics card and minutes while it is busy. Saved workflows keep their choice.

### Fixed

- **Plenio Nova's visuals flickered at their edges and in their finest detail**, most of all the Mandelbulb, Spectrum City and the Mandala. What is finer than a pixel is now drawn as its even glow: the Mandelbulb's surface and its outline, Spectrum City's windows, roof neon and street lanes, the Mandala's finest threads, Outrun's grid far off, the streaks of Hyperspace and the glitter on the moonlit sea; the glow no longer blinks with a single bright pixel, and the fine grain stands still. Measured from frame to frame at the same size and with the same music, the flicker is down by 73 % in the Mandala, 51 % in the Mandelbulb, 50 % around the black hole and 31 % in Spectrum City.
- **A strip of another picture at the top and right edge of a Plenio Nova visual**, most visible while one visual turned into the next: scaling the picture onto the screen blended in what was drawn beside it. The picture now has an edge of its own to blend with.
- **Spectrum City flew through its towers**; they now stay below the flight.
- **Plenio Nova's visuals were missing from *Fixed***: a visual is offered once it has been timed smooth on the graphics card, and one timed while the card was busy - a song being made on it - stayed out. Every visual is timed anew with this version, one timed too heavy again each time the player opens (keeping its faster timing), and the lists of one visual - *Fixed*, this song's, the playlist's - show the ones not offered here greyed, with why (*being timed on this graphics card …*, *too heavy for this graphics card*).
- **A section the writer filled with what the band plays was sung**: lines that describe the playing - an instrument and how it is played, or *no words, just sound* - without brackets, went into the lyrics, and the music model sang them. A section made mostly of such lines keeps its tag without lines - an instrumental part - and the writers are told that a solo or a break is its tag alone; a Song Sheet warns of such a section. A single such line among sung ones stays, as it may be meant to be sung.

## 1.4.0 - 2026-10-29

**Visuals worked out for every pixel, stars for every song, and a say in what the party hears.** Plenio Nova brings 22 visuals the graphics card works out for every pixel - a black hole, a galaxy, northern lights over a lake, the Mandelbulb, liquid chrome -, in a choice that stays compact with a hundred visuals, and the player offers only the visuals a device draws smoothly. Every song gets stars from 0 to 5: yours by ear, and the measured ones as a faded pre-fill - how much of the lyrics is sung as written, how clean the harmony is, how steady the beat, and what went wrong. Users listen, make songs or help as admins; a new song can wait until its maker heard it or an admin approved it, and the guests hear the party's songs again on their phones. Videos come in seven quality steps or at a bitrate of your own. The player starts ComfyUI the way you last started it and names a model file ComfyUI does not find; a song's page says when, how long and on what it was made. The guests' reactions are kept for every song; only the guests' songs are party songs, and the big screen says which party. Videos give their graphics memory back and try again. The lyrics are there in full to copy again - on the song's page in the player and on a page of their own beside every song - and YuE2 takes LoRAs.

### Added

- **The full lyrics to copy**: *Full lyrics*, beside *As written* and *As sung* on a song's page in the player, shows the lyrics as text with their sections (`[Verse]`, `[Chorus]` …) - what goes into a Song Sheet - with buttons that copy the lyrics, the lyrics without the section tags and the style, and the lyrics as the take sings them below. Before, a song with measured times showed its lyrics only as timed lines. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#a-songs-page)
- **The song's page** (`<name>.html`): Export Release writes everything about the song on one page beside it - the cover and the song to play, the lyrics with their sections, the style, the brief and the cover's prompt with copy buttons, every field of the brief, how it was made (the model files, the LoRAs, the seeds), the sound and its mastering, the score as ABC and links to every file. A double click opens it in any browser, without internet. *song page (HTML)* on the node, on by default; the player shows the same page for every song of its library, also one exported before. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#the-songs-page-html)
- **YuE2 LoRAs**: a node under the music model of the YuE2 templates with up to four LoRAs, each with its strength. It reads from each file whether it shapes YuE2's plan (the planner, CLIP) or its sound (the acoustic model, MODEL) and applies it there; files in m-a-p's original layout - which ComfyUI's own LoRA loader skips - are joined into ComfyUI's fused projections - block-diagonally, `down = [A_q; A_k; A_v]` and `up = diag(B_q, B_k, B_v)`, so `up · down` is exactly the stacked deltas of q, k and v (and of gate and up). A file that does not fit - another model's LoRA, a broken file, weights of another shape or values that are not numbers - is left out and named, and the rest applies; with every slot on *none*, as the templates ship, YuE2 runs as loaded. LoRA training stays outside Plenio for now. [Details](docs/user/concepts/loras.md)
- **Song API 1.2**: the release field `song_page`.
- **How a song was made, on its page**: the date and time, how long making it took, the graphics card (with its memory) or the processor alone, the processor and the RAM, and the lyrics fidelity. Export Release writes them into the release record (`made`; the names of the graphics card and the processor, no serial number, user name or path - [what the record holds](SECURITY.md#plenios-http-routes)); songs exported before show when they were saved and, where their record says it, the fidelity. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#a-songs-page)
- **The guests' reactions kept for every song**, apart from the favourites' hearts: counted in the player's state (`reactions.json`), shown in Live Play and on the song's page (*🔥 12 · ❤️ 4 from the guests*). The host chooses **as many per song as they like** or **one per song and guest** - a tap on another emoji changes it. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#guests-in-your-network)
- The example songs as videos made by the Plenio Player, in the README (*Listen first*) and the getting-started guide; tutorials 9 and 10 in the README's table.
- **The demo page shows Plenio at work**: two films with sound - *Plenio in 30 seconds* (the player playing one song, in step with its lyrics, between ComfyUI's brief, Song Sheet, score editor and EQ and a party's big screen) and *Twelve genres in thirty seconds* -, the song videos, new songs and the archive of more than seventy songs and covers. New songs and song videos are added with `tools/demo_showcase.py` from a song's FLAC or record - its cover, genre, length, tempo, key, style, lyrics and how it was made, nothing else of the record - with SoundCloud, YouTube or its MP3 to play. [How](docs/demo-showcase.md)
- **Plenio Nova: 22 visuals the graphics card works out for every pixel** - flights through hyperspace, neon rings, a city whose towers are the spectrum and an outrun valley; a black hole bending the starlight round its disk, a galaxy, a nebula, northern lights over a lake, the sea under the moon; the Mandelbulb, gyroid caves, liquid chrome, a sound sphere, a plasma globe, stained glass, a laser show, a mandala, silk; and three that grow from frame to frame - ink in water, living patterns (a reaction-diffusion), the song's last seconds as ridge lines (*Pulsar*) and as mountains. Most are ray-marched in 3D; all move with the bass, its kicks, the mids, the highs, the beat, the spectrum and the waveform, take the colour scheme as their palette and go through the same light: a bloom, a filmic tone curve (ACES), a touch of colour fringing on the beat, a vignette and grain. They play live, in the video gallery's previews and in videos. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#visuals)

The dialog says what a setting means in Mbit/s and MB a minute. The picture stays H.264, which every player and platform plays. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#save-a-song-as-a-video)
- **Groups of users**: a user *listens* (may not make songs), *makes songs*, or is an *admin* - a helper with the admin's rights from any device (users and their groups, approvals, the party's settings), to step in when needed; their own playlists and likes stay theirs, and what only the player's computer does stays the computer's. Users set up before are makers. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#who-is-listening-the-login)

A party begins when the guests' access is switched on, gets a new address, or the host starts a new one. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#guests-in-your-network)
- **Stars for every song, 0 to 5**: on a song's page in the player, the stars you give by ear (in halves; kept like your playlists, each user their own) and the measured ones - faded, a pre-fill until you give yours: how much of the lyrics as written is sung, how clean the harmony is (the notes against the chords, clashes a semitone apart, one key), how steady the beat, and what takes stars off (an ending cut off, silent holes, a song much shorter than asked, clipping, a loudness off its aim, the Song Sheet's warnings). *Details* lists every measure. - **Tutorial 11 · YuE2 LoRAs**: the node, the two halves of YuE2 a LoRA shapes, a LoRA chosen with its strength, an instrumental made with it and what the node says after the run (`tools/tutorials/yue2-loras.mjs`). ### Security

- **What only this computer may ask is answered only on it.** ComfyUI has no login, and with `--listen` - or the player's *In your network* - everyone in the network reaches Plenio's routes in it. The System Check's report, which names the computer's folders and versions, and starting the Plenio Player now answer only a browser on the computer ComfyUI runs on; the System Check node still shows the report in the workflow. [Details](SECURITY.md#plenios-http-routes)
- **An error tells the network no more than that it happened**: a bug in one of Plenio's routes or the Song API is answered without its text, which can name a folder of the computer - ComfyUI's console keeps it. The player answers requests from the network alike, and names them no place of the computer: folders and files only by their names.
- **Only the admin and the admins' group take songs out of the library**: listeners, makers and guests keep their own playlists, not the songs, and *Remove…* shows only to whom may.
- **The player and Local LLM start only what they are meant to**: the studio's folder must be a ComfyUI folder (its `main.py` beside `folder_paths.py`, `execution.py` and `comfy`) and its remembered Python a Python; a `llama-server` named in the settings or in `PLENIO_LLAMA_SERVER` must be one, or another program is not started. An address of a page's own to look for ComfyUI at is taken only from this computer.
