Plenio VST3

The Plenio Music Production System in your DAW: songs and covers made on your own computer, up to four takes per run, stems, and the words, melody and chords to change and render again.

Experimental on macOS. This version is built for macOS but has not been tried on a Mac yet. Tell us how it went: github.com/jplenio/Plenio-Music-Production-System/issues.

It needs a DAW that loads VST3 instruments (REAPER, Cubase, Nuendo, Studio One, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig Studio …; Logic Pro loads Audio Units only), macOS 13.3 or newer, and, to make songs, ComfyUI with Plenio 1.6 or newer.

YuE2 and SheetSage2, the models that make the songs, are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0: what you make with them is for non-commercial use.

The installer puts Plenio.vst3 into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
