Plenio VST3 is installed.

Next
1. Open your DAW and let it look for new plug-ins (REAPER: Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan;
   most DAWs do it on their own at start).
2. Put "Plenio" on a new instrument track and open its window.
3. Start ComfyUI with Plenio (or let Plenio start it: Settings), then New song.
