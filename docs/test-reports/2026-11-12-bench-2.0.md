## How it was measured

- **Code:** the working tree of 2.0.0 at `0c3ba78` (phases 0 to 8.5; `pyproject.toml` still says 1.6.0, the version line above is the package's). ComfyUI 0.44.0, the second card (RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1`), `tools/dev_server.py --gpu` with the model folder of this computer, every model field on `auto`, automatic downloads off. Briefs one after another, three runs each, the seeds of `tools/bench.py`.
- **Not idle:** during the first run of `sung-180-hq-cover` and parts of `sung-90` the agent ran the unit tests on the same computer (all processor threads for about 20 s at a time); the first run of `sung-180-hq-cover` (692 s, finishing 545 s) is slower for that reason. Runs 2 and 3 of that brief ran with light work only.
- **Not heard:** the songs were not listened to. **Not run:** the cover brief (it needs a recording that may be used).

## Before and after

Phase 8 changed nothing a run spends time on (see below: no hypothesis measured a gain above 5 % of a run on this card, and 8.6 changes what the user is told, not the time), so this report is both the *before* and the *after* of phase 8.

**Against 1.6.0 the medians are 5 to 11 % longer, and the songs are of other lengths.** With the same seeds 2.0 makes songs of other lengths (its model plan, writer and templates differ): seconds of song per run `instrumental-60` [59, 60, 59] (1.6.0: [60, 53, 62]), `sung-90` [136, 199, 138] (1.6.0: [146, 151, 112]), `sung-180-hq-cover` [222, 193, 204] (1.6.0: [175, 177, 193]). Per second of song every brief is as fast or faster (the column above); the finishing stage of `sung-180-hq-cover` takes 2.2 s per second of song on both versions (runs 2 and 3). `tools/bench.py --compare` and `--check` therefore judge *slower* per second of song where both reports know their songs' lengths. **No brief is slower than on 1.6.0** by that measure; by the raw median `sung-180-hq-cover` is 10.7 % longer, which its longer songs explain (median 204 s of song against 177 s, 15 % more). `sung-90`'s spread is the widest: 123 to 174 s with the same seeds, as on 1.6.0 (114 to 182 s).
