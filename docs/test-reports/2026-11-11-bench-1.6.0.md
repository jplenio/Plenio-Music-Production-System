## How it was measured

- **Code:** the working tree at `ae03b65` - 1.6.0 (`c307d35`) plus phase 0.1 (the stage timings this report reads; 1.6.0 itself writes none), phase 0.5 (tests only) and phase 1 (the package boundary, route classes). None of them changes what a song's generation does; the numbers stand for 1.6.0.
- **Machine:** ComfyUI 0.44.0, the second card (RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1`), a dev server (`tools/dev_server.py --gpu`) with the model folder of this computer, automatic downloads off. Briefs one after another, three runs each, fixed seeds (`SEEDS` in `tools/bench.py`).
- **Not run:** the fourth brief of PD §6.1, a cover: it needs a recording (`--source`) that may be used for it. Not heard: the songs were not listened to; the benchmark measures time only.

## What it shows

- **Finishing is the largest stage of a sung song:** 84.5 s of 153.5 s at 1:30, 397.2 s of 534.3 s at 3:00 with HQ mastering - more than writing, score and render together. Phase 8 starts there.
- **The first run of a brief is slower** by about 30 s (instrumental, sung 1:30), but the stages' *load* is 0.0-0.9 s. Either the time goes to loading the timings do not catch (models a node loads by itself, outside ComfyUI's model management), or to something else on a cold start. To be found out before phase 8 reads *load* as small.
- **Runs differ a lot:** `sung-90` took 181.8, 153.5 and 113.8 s with the same seeds; `sung-180-hq-cover` 516.2, 534.3 and 567.8 s. The release check's 10 % tolerance is narrower than this spread on one machine; three runs may not be enough to call a brief slower. To be judged against the run-to-run spread when 2.0 is compared.
- **Peak memory** stays at 2.3-6.5 GiB on the 16 GB card for these briefs.
