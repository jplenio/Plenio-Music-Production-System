### L1 - Refine (`sr_study.py`), material: a 48 kHz YuE2 take (reference) and the same MiniMax take (target)

Simulation set (the reference band-limited at 14.5 kHz; LSD compares the 16-24 kHz band with the reference):

| Arm | LSD 16-24 kHz | fizz flatness | loudness change | true peak | RTF | peak VRAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| resample only | 113.39 dB | 0.153 | 0.000 LU | -0.842 dBTP | 121.7 | 220 MB |
| UniverSR (released weights) | **11.00 dB** | 0.463 | 0.000 LU | -0.836 dBTP | 0.41 | 2 776 MB |

Target set (the real MiniMax take, no reference):

| Arm | RTF | fizz flatness | loudness change | true peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| resample only | 47.47 | 0.0007 | -0.062 LU | -0.37 dBTP |
| UniverSR | 47.25 | 0.0007 | -0.062 LU | -0.37 dBTP |

**Both target numbers are identical because the model never ran**: the stage's report says
`"input already full band (22050 Hz) - resample only"`. The bandwidth rule (the highest frequency within
60 dB of the 1-4 kHz level) reads both real takes as full band - the MiniMax take at its Nyquist
(22 050 Hz), the YuE2 take at 21 949 Hz. An independent long-term spectrum shows why the rule is
permissive (levels relative to the 1-4 kHz band):

| Band | MiniMax take (44.1 kHz) | YuE2 take (48 kHz) |
|---|---|---|
| 10-14 kHz | -15.4 dB | -11.5 dB |
| 16-18 kHz | -27.5 dB | -21.3 dB |
| 18-20 kHz | -33.0 dB | -42.2 dB |
| 20-22 kHz | -37.8 dB | -54.2 dB |
| 22-24 kHz | (above Nyquist) | -69.2 dB |

So the MiniMax take is **not** brick-walled at ~14.5 kHz (the plan's "~32-kHz-class signal" assumption);
it decays smoothly and keeps low-level content to Nyquist, while the YuE2 take falls off a cliff above
~19 kHz. **Consequence:** with the shipped defaults, Refine's model engine adds nothing to these takes;
whether the MiniMax template should keep the model engine, force it (a stricter bandwidth rule) or drop
the block is a **design decision for the owner**, to be made on the blind material - not changed silently
here. Blind A/B material (band-limited input vs UniverSR, level-matched, fixed-seed order): 
`D:/Daten2/ComfyUI/output/plenio-l1-pack/2026-09-27 Words We Never Said (2) (original)/` with `key.json`
and `README.md`.

### What the numbers do not say

- **Nothing about how either stage sounds.** The listening verdicts (L1, L2) are the owner's; the
  decision rule of plan §4.5 (>= 70 % preference on >= 10 takes) needs more takes than the one measured.
- Separation quality itself (bleed, artefacts, the *other* bus) was not measured against a reference;
  the authors' MUSDB18-HQ SDR 9.66 is quoted, not re-measured.
- One take per statement; no claim is generalised from it.

### Update 2026-09-28 (the owner's first real use)

The numbers above are the 2026-09-27 run. After the owner's review the suites stand at **946**
(unit + workflow + contract), **113** (host) and **158 Vitest** in 16 files; `pytest` and
`npm run check` are green, ruff/format clean, mypy with the same 44 `type-arg` errors as before.
What changed since the first run:

- the **stem order** of *Separate Stems* is now the checkpoint's own (`drums, bass, other, vocals`;
  the hardcoded order mislabelled every stem - a defect only a listening check could find);
- a **canonical guide form** for MIDI (overlapping notes of one pitch cannot round-trip);
- the **Song Sheet window** resizes and fills the browser window, and its panes drag;
- the templates carry the owner's **layout** (ported into `tools/build_graphs.py`);
- the score layout's two cosmetic defects (the track rows' wrapped cells and the panes' second grid
  row) were reproduced and fixed with a **scratch Chromium probe** (a bare Vite page mounting the
  score tab in a dialog-sized wrapper, read back as boxes): the track rows measure 34 px with no
  overlap and `count` in its own 36-41 px cell, and `.side`, the splitter, `.score-views` and the
  inspector share one 280 px grid row in the review, DAW and text layouts. The probe was not kept -
  it is a measurement scratch tool, not a test; the interactions still belong to L4;
- L1 ran again with the owner's real **32 kHz-class MiniMax file** as the reference (LSD 97.1 dB ->
  34.6 dB; the model still reports "input already full band" for the file itself), and the L2
  material was rewritten for the owner's **better take** (`plenio-l2/2026-09-26 Neuer Morgen Jetzt (original)/`).

The listening verdicts (L1, L2) remain the owner's; `is_experimental` stays on until they are in.
