## 2. Rules (from the legacy toolkit's measured prompts)

- Caption: three headed parts - *Global Metadata* (`bpm is ... key is ..., and scale is ...` plus genre), *Vocal Details*, *Arrangement*; about 300 words; no section tags, no ABC, no lyric lines. A free-text caption is accepted with an info note.
- Instrumental: *Vocal Details* exactly `n/a` (Parse sets it; the sheet refuses anything else); the lyrics are a tags-only map about twice as long as a sung song's (`[Intro]`, `[Instrumental]` ..., `[Solo]`, `[Break]`, `[Outro]`), because MiniMax ends short maps early; vocal tags such as `[Chorus]` warn.
- Lyrics length: about one line every 5 s (warn outside 0.5-2x).
- **I-5 (instrumental caption wording)**: the legacy wording is adopted; Plenio's own listening check needs real MiniMax renders (owner's machine).
