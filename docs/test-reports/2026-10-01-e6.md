## 1. The owner's records (offline, read only)

274 instrumental YuE2 songs with a rendered score (Plenio 0.2.2-0.3.0): 22 % within 0.8-1.2 x the brief's length,
45 % longer (61 above 2 x, up to 3.8 x), 33 % shorter. The long ones are mostly an intro plus one section of 400-850 s.
*fit length* of 0.3.1 applied to their scores: 92 % within 0.8-1.2 x (unit-tested, `tests/unit/test_fit_length.py`).

Audio variety of their 301 instrumental files (metric below): over-long songs (> 1.5 x) 0.19, songs within the
length 0.64; by style EDM 0.42, electronic 0.40, cinematic 0.67, classical 0.78.

**Metric** (`audio_variety`): 4-s windows, chroma and MFCC means; a window is *novel* when its cosine similarity to
every earlier window except its neighbour stays below 0.985 (or 0.97); also the spread of the windows' loudness (dB).
A proxy for monotony, not a listening verdict; it falls with length, so takes are compared at the same length.

## 4. Decisions

- Keep the 0.3.1 form; do not add arrangement words to the style, do not plan with the adapter.
- Render lyrics stay `[instrumental]` (no section tags in the render - the owner's verdict of Phase 4A).
- The owner's blind listening pack (outside the repository): 10 A/B pairs (`[instrumental]` vs the form, order random,
  key in a separate file) and the two takes Check Vocals flagged.
