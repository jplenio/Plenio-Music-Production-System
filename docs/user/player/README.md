# Plenio Player - the guide

A page in your browser that plays the songs you made with Plenio - a program of its own, with or without ComfyUI.

- [Listening](listening.md) - Open the player, find your songs, a song's page, Now playing, playlists, keys.
- [Visuals](visuals.md) - The pictures that move with the music: classics, Plenio Drop, Plenio Nova.
- [Party](party.md) - Live Play and your guests: the live playlist, the party's page on their phones.
- [Making songs](making-songs.md) - Songs made from the player - by you, your people and your guests.
- [People & access](people.md) - Who comes in and what each may do: your devices, a party, people with accounts.
- [Sharing](sharing.md) - Your songs as a player of their own, in one file.
- [Videos](videos.md) - A song as a video, with its visual and its lyrics.

For programs: the [Player API](../../api/player.md) (remote controls, stream decks, home automation).
