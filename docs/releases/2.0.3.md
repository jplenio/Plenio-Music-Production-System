# Plenio 2.0.3

**No folder of this computer in a node's output, an album shared as a player keeps its running order, sharing from a large library starts from the songs in view, and Plenio VST3 says when ComfyUI runs without Plenio.**

> **New to Plenio?** Plenio is a free, open-source song production suite for ComfyUI that runs on your own PC: type an idea and get a finished, mastered song with cover art, timed lyrics and sheet music - sung by YuE2 or MiniMax Music 3. See and change every word, note and chord before it is sung, and play it to a room from the Plenio Player. [Listen first](https://jplenio.github.io/Plenio-Music-Production-System/) · [README](https://github.com/jplenio/Plenio-Music-Production-System#readme) · [Guide](https://jplenio.github.io/Plenio-Music-Production-System/guide/)

## Install

- **ComfyUI Manager**: search for *Plenio Music Production System* - or `comfy node install comfyui-plenio-music`.
- **The full download**: `git clone https://github.com/jplenio/Plenio-Music-Production-System` into `ComfyUI/custom_nodes`, or the zip below. It also carries the Studio add-on: the player starts ComfyUI, a `llama-server` runs a GGUF writer, *Open the folder* opens the file manager.

Restart ComfyUI: **Plenio Setup** opens and plans the model files for your graphics card.

## Every change

<details><summary><b>Added</b> (2)</summary>

- **Share as a player in your order.** The dialog numbers the songs chosen and lets you arrange them: drag a song or move it with the arrows, or sort by album and track number (from the songs' tags), by title or the other way round. The shared player lists and plays them in that order, so an album keeps its running order when someone opens the file; *In order (as shared)* in its sort list brings the order back after the listener has sorted otherwise. Before, the songs went in the library's order and the shared player started with the newest first.
- **Share as a player from thousands of songs.** The dialog's list shows the songs you opened it with - a view with its search and filters, a playlist - instead of the whole library; *Choose from* switches to all songs, the favourites or a playlist. Its search takes the library's words (`#tag`, `bpm:>100`, `is:fav` ...), *tick these* ticks every song the list shows, and the list grows a page at a time, so a library of 3000 songs opens at once.

</details>

<details><summary><b>Security</b> (3)</summary>

- **System Check names no folder of this computer in its output.** ComfyUI keeps every node's output in `/history`, which everyone who reaches ComfyUI reads (with `--listen`, everyone in the network). The System Check's report named the models folder, the asset and LLM folders and misplaced files by their absolute paths, and its full report added the settings with their folders. It now names a folder by its last two parts (`…/models/vae`), the home folder as `~` and the user's name as `<user>`, and leaves the settings and folders out of the raw facts; Plenio Setup still shows them in full, on this computer only.
- **An unexpected error in a node's output is named without its place.** Export Release (quality, song page, vocal stem), Apply Arrangement, Fit Lyrics, Match Song Form and Sung Pitch go on after an unexpected error and say so in their output; they now give the error's kind and text with every path cut to its last two parts.
- **Hardening.** The *Plenio Player* button passes the player ComfyUI's own address instead of the request's `Host` header. More proxy headers (`X-Forwarded-Host`, `X-Forwarded-Proto`, `X-Client-IP`, `True-Client-IP`, `CF-Connecting-IP`, `Via`) mark a request as one from the network. The fallback LLM server answers no web page: no request with an `Origin`, a `Host` other than its loopback address, or a POST that is not JSON. Answers to the network also take the home folder and the user's name out of a path with a space.

</details>

<details><summary><b>Fixed</b> (1)</summary>

- **Plenio VST3 says when ComfyUI runs without Plenio.** A ComfyUI that answers on its port but has no Plenio loaded - not installed in a portable ComfyUI started by its own `.bat`, or one whose import failed - was reported as "ComfyUI with Plenio is not running", which read as if the plug-in did not see the running ComfyUI at all. The plug-in now says that ComfyUI (with its version and port) runs without Plenio, and what to do: install Plenio there and restart ComfyUI, or look for *IMPORT FAILED* in its window.

</details>
