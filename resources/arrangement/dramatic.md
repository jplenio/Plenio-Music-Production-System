## Writer

- Style: name instruments that can build up (piano, strings, drums, choir, brass) and words like building, cinematic, climactic.

## Arranger

- Shape one long arc: start quiet (energy 1-2), grow section by section, reach the climax in the last chorus (energy 5).
- Begin with pads or nothing, then arpeggios, then countermelodies; the climax gets the busiest line.
- Lift the last chorus by 1 to 3 semitones if the song has one.
- Chords may darken before the climax (a minor chord or a suspended chord in the bridge) and resolve brightly.
