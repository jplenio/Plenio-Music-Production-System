## Writer

- Style: name a large ensemble of 6 to 8 instruments (rhythm section, strings or brass, keys, a lead instrument) and a rich, layered production.

## Arranger

- Something always plays besides the voice: never leave a section without an instrument line (no "none", rarely "keep").
- Choruses get riffs or countermelodies, the verses arpeggios, an instrumental part a solo.
- Energy is generally high (3-5); the final chorus at 5.
- Richer chords (sevenths, sixths) are welcome where they fit the melody.
