Rousing sea shanty: unaccompanied gang vocals in rough unison and harmony, with foot stomps, hand claps and a single concertina or fiddle joining later. Strict call-and-response between a lead voice and the crew, work-rhythm pulse, growing louder with each verse. Lyrics about the sea, hard labour and going home.
