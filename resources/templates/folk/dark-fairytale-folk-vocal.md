Enchanting and slightly eerie folk: soft close vocals, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, harp, cello, distant flute and restrained hand percussion. Beautiful recurring modal melody with a mysterious fairytale atmosphere. Lyrics about entering an ancient forest where memories, dreams and reality slowly become indistinguishable. Romantic, poetic and haunting rather than frightening.
