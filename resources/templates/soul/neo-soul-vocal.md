Neo-soul: loose behind-the-beat drums with ghost notes, warm round bass, lush Rhodes chords with extended jazz harmony, muted guitar and layered improvised vocal stacks. Relaxed, organic and harmonically rich, with a floating wordless outro. Lyrics about self-worth, patience and choosing yourself.
