Classic 1960s Motown soul: driving tambourine and backbeat drums, melodic walking bass, bright piano and vibraphone, punchy horn stabs, sweeping strings and a joyful lead vocal answered by a tight female backing trio. Simple irresistible hook, warm vintage mono-flavoured mix. Lyrics about falling in love and not being able to hide it.
