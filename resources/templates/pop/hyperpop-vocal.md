Maximalist hyperpop: pitched-up chipmunk vocals, distorted 808 bass, glitchy edits, blown-out saturated drums, sugary supersaw chords and abrupt beat switches. Extremely loud, chaotic and euphoric, with an unexpectedly emotional bridge. Very short repetitive hook. Lyrics about being online, overstimulated and in love.
