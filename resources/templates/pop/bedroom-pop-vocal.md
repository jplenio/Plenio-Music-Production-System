Lo-fi bedroom pop: slightly detuned chorus guitar, warm tape-saturated synths, soft muffled drums, simple bass and a close, breathy double-tracked vocal. Hazy, intimate and imperfect, with vinyl noise and a woozy pitch drift. Lyrics about lying awake, overthinking and small hopes.
