Classic power ballad: gentle piano and clean guitar intro, softly sung verse, strings entering in the second verse, full band and huge distorted guitars in the final chorus, a soaring guitar solo and a key change before the last chorus. Enormous emotional dynamic range. Lyrics about holding on to someone across distance and time.
