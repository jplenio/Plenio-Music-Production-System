Deadpan comedy rap over a hard boom-bap beat: dusty drums, gritty bass, simple looped sample. Confident, aggressive delivery and elaborate braggadocio about something completely mundane, with increasingly ludicrous punchlines and one repeated chant hook. The joke is the contrast between the serious beat and the trivial subject.
