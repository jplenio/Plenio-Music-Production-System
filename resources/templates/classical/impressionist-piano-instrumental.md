Impressionist solo piano in the spirit of Debussy and Ravel: floating whole-tone and modal harmony, shimmering pedal-blurred arpeggios, gentle rubato and a wandering melody that never quite resolves. Watery, dreamlike and delicate, with soft dynamics and a natural salon-piano tone.
