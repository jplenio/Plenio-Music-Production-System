Folk metal: heavy distorted riffs interlocked with fiddle, tin whistle and accordion playing a joyful folk melody, driving drums and a rough shouted lead with a huge drunken gang-vocal chorus. Accelerating dance section in the middle, festival energy. Lyrics about feasts, journeys and old legends.
