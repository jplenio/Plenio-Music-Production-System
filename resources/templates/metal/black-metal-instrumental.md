Atmospheric black metal: cold tremolo-picked tremolo guitar walls, relentless blast beats, buried bass and distant icy synth pads. Repetitive hypnotic minor melodies, raw lo-fi production, a long ambient middle section with clean guitar, then a return to the freezing storm.
