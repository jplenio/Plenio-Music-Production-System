Brutal death metal: heavily downtuned tremolo-picked riffs, blast beats and rapid double-kick, thick distorted bass and deep guttural growled vocals. Frequent tempo shifts, dissonant harmony, a crushing slow breakdown and a chaotic sweep-picked solo. Dense, dark and uncompromising.
