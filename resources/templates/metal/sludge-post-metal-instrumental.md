Sludge / post-metal: enormous slow fuzzed-out riffs, feedback, thick bass and thunderous drums, alternating with long clean atmospheric passages of tremolo guitar and reverb. Very gradual crescendo structure, oceanic and crushing, emotional rather than aggressive.
