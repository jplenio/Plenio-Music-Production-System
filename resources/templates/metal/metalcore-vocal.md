Modern metalcore: chugging downtuned riffs, melodic lead guitar over screamed verses, a soaring clean-sung chorus, tight double-kick drums and a heavy half-time breakdown at the end. Sharp modern production, strong contrast between aggression and melody. Lyrics about inner struggle, betrayal and fighting back.
