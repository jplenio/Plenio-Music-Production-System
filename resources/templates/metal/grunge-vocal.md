Raw, heavy grunge: dirty distorted guitars with loose bends, thick bass, powerful sloppy drums and a rough expressive vocal moving between mumbled verses and a screamed chorus. Simple but unforgettable riff, extreme quiet-loud dynamics, unpolished early-1990s production. Lyrics about frustration, disillusionment and feeling trapped between anger and apathy.
