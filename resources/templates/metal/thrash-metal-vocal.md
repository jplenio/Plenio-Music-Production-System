Fast, aggressive thrash metal: razor-sharp palm-muted riffs, relentless double-kick and fast snare, energetic bass and an intense shouted vocal. Abrupt tempo changes, a half-time breakdown, wild whammy-bar solo and a precise machine-gun rhythm section. Raw and rhythmically precise. Lyrics about chaos, corruption and loss of control.
