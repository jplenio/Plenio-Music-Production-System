Dramatic symphonic metal: heavy guitars and double-kick drums combined with full orchestral strings, brass and choir textures, plus a strong operatic-leaning female lead vocal. Memorable melodic themes, huge cinematic chorus, dynamic contrast with a quiet orchestral bridge and a soaring guitar solo. Lyrics about fate, loss and finding light within darkness.
