Deeply emotional cinematic underscore: simple felt-piano motif, gradually joined by solo cello, warm string bed and a distant solo flute. Very slow build to a single restrained climax, then a bare piano ending. Intimate, tender and heartbreaking rather than epic. Natural close-mic sound.
