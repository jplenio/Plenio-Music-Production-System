New Orleans Dixieland: collective improvisation between clarinet, cornet and trombone, with banjo, tuba or slap bass and a snare-driven two-beat. Joyful polyphony, breaks and stop-time choruses, a rousing final ride-out chorus. Raw, jubilant vintage recording.
