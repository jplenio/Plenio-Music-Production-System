Cool, restrained modal jazz: muted trumpet stating a spare lyrical melody, tenor sax, understated piano voicings, upright bass and brushed drums. Slow harmonic rhythm over one or two modes, plenty of space, soft dynamics and an introspective late-1950s atmosphere.
