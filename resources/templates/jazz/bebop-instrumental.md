Fast bebop quintet: trumpet and alto sax in unison on an intricate angular head, then blistering improvised solos over rapid ii-V changes, comping piano, walking bass and busy brushless drums with snare bombs. Head-solos-head form, live club atmosphere, raw energy and virtuosity.
