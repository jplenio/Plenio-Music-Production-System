Deeply emotional Portuguese fado: Portuguese guitarra with its shimmering tremolo lines, classical guitar and bass viola, and a dramatic heartbroken vocal full of rubato and sudden dynamic swells. No drums, minor key, aching saudade. Lyrics about fate, the sea and missing someone.
