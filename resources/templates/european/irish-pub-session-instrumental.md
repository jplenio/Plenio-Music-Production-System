Lively Irish pub session: fiddle and tin whistle playing a reel in unison, bouncing bodhrán, strummed DADGAD guitar, button accordion and occasional uilleann pipes. Traditional two-tune set with repeats, accelerating energy, hand claps and a raw live-room recording.
