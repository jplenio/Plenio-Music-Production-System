High-energy Balkan brass band: blaring trumpets in tight harmony, oompah tuba bass, snare and booming tupan drums, odd-meter grooves and virtuosic unison runs. Starts as a slow rubato lament, then accelerates into a frenzied, joyful, unstoppably danceable finale.
