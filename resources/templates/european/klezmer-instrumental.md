Expressive and festive klezmer: wailing clarinet lead with bent notes and krekhts sobs, over accordion, violin, upright bass and driving frame drum. Laughing-and-crying phrases in a freygish mode, a slow doina-style intro then an accelerating wedding-dance finale.
