Passionate and virtuosic flamenco: fast rasgueado and picado nylon-string guitar, palmas handclaps, cajón, zapateado footwork and soaring Phrygian melodic lines. Free rubato falseta passages alternating with driving compás sections, dramatic pauses, intense Andalusian spirit.
