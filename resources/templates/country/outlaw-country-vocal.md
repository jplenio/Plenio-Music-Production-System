Gritty outlaw country: twangy Telecaster, weeping pedal steel, walking bass, brushed shuffle drums and a weathered, world-worn baritone vocal. Open, honest arrangement, live-room warmth, storytelling verses and a plain-spoken chorus. Lyrics about the road, bad decisions and refusing to be tamed.
