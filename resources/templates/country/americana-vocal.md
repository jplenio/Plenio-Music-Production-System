Warm Americana / roots song: layered acoustic and slide guitars, dobro, organ pads, upright bass, loose brushed drums and a plainspoken vocal with close male-female harmony. Organic, dusty, unpolished production with plenty of space. Lyrics about leaving a place behind and looking for something better down the road.
