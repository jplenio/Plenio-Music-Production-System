Classic honky-tonk: shuffling two-beat drums, walking upright bass, barroom piano, crying pedal steel and twangy lead guitar, with a nasal traditional country vocal. Vintage 1960s production, call-and-response fills between steel and guitar, danceable and unpretentious. Lyrics about heartbreak, cheap beer and a jukebox.
