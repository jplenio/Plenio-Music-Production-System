Hypnotic, warm cumbia: shuffling güira and guacharaca percussion, deep bouncing bass, accordion melodies, bright trumpet lines and tremolo guitar. Laid-back but danceable, dreamy sunlit Latin atmosphere, repeating melodic hook with call-and-response between accordion and horns.
