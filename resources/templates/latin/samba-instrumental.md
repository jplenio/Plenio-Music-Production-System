Joyful Brazilian samba batucada: surdo heartbeat, tamborim and repinique patterns, cuíca accents, agogô bells, bright cavaquinho comping, seven-string guitar and melodic horns. Driving carnival spirit, tight call-and-response breaks and an irresistible sense of forward movement.
