Classic son cubano: tres guitar tumbao, upright bass anticipating the beat, bongó and claves, subtle trumpet obbligato and a warm lead voice answered by a coro. Relaxed clave swing, verse section leading into a montuno call-and-response, vintage Havana recording warmth. Lyrics about home, a woman and the passing of time.
