Hard, euphoric hardstyle: distorted pitched-down kick with a heavy tail, screeching reverse bass, aggressive lead screeches and a euphoric melodic breakdown with piano and strings. Massive build with a long riser, then a hard-hitting drop. Raw energy, loud and heavily compressed.
