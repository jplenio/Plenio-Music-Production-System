Post-rock crescendo: clean delay-and-reverb guitars building layered arpeggios over a slow hypnotic bass and gradually intensifying drums, joined by strings and tremolo-picked guitar walls in a cathartic climax before falling back to near-silence. Instrumental throughout, no verse-chorus form, one long emotional arc.
