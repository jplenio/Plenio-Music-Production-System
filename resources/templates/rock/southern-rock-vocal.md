Southern rock: twin harmonized lead guitars, slide guitar, boogie piano, thick bass and swinging drums, with a gritty soulful vocal and a gospel-flavoured backing chorus. Long dual-guitar solo section building to a euphoric finish. Warm, live and organic. Lyrics about home, the highway and hard-won pride.
