Cheerful retro Christmas pop: sleigh bells, brushed shuffle drums, walking bass, jazzy piano, warm horns, glockenspiel and a smooth crooning vocal with a big backing-vocal chorus. Nostalgic 1960s festive production, key change before the last chorus. Lyrics about coming home for the holidays and snow outside the window.
