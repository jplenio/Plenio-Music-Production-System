High-energy Bhangra: booming dhol double-headed drum pattern, tumbi twang riff, algoza flute, hand claps, deep modern bass and bright synth stabs. Relentless celebratory groove, call-and-response shouts used rhythmically, joyous and irresistibly danceable.
