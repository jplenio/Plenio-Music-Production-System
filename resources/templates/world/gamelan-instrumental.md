Balinese gamelan: interlocking kotekan patterns on bronze metallophones and gangsa, resonant gongs marking the cycle, kendang drum leading sudden tempo and dynamic changes, shimmering detuned tuning. Cyclical structure, explosive unison accents, hypnotic and ceremonial.
