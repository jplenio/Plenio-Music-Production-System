Mongolian steppe music: deep khoomei overtone throat singing over morin khuur horsehead fiddle, tovshuur lute and a slow galloping frame-drum pulse. Wide, windswept open-plain atmosphere, pentatonic melodies, ancient and hypnotic, with long sustained drones.
