Modern Arabic pop: oud and qanun melodic lines over a driving darbuka and riq rhythm, lush unison strings playing ornamented maqam phrases, synth bass and modern drums. Melismatic passionate vocal, big singable chorus, glossy production. Lyrics about love, distance and devotion.
