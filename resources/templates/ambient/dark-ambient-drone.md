Dark ambient drone: deep sub-bass drones, slowly shifting dissonant textures, metallic resonances, distant processed field recordings and granular noise. No beat, no melody in the conventional sense; tension builds through density and slow filter movement. Cold, vast, unsettling and cinematic.
