Trance-like Saharan desert blues: droning electric guitar with hypnotic pentatonic riffs, loping camel-walk groove, hand percussion, calabash and hand claps, and a raw soulful vocal with call-and-response chants. Repetitive, wide open-space atmosphere, minimal chord changes, warm analog tone. Lyrics about long journeys, home and freedom.
