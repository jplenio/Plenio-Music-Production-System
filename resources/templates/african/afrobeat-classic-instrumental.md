Classic 1970s Nigerian Afrobeat in the Fela Kuti tradition: long hypnotic groove built on tight interlocking guitar and clavinet riffs, deep repetitive bass, busy congas and shekere, a punchy tenor-sax and trumpet horn section with call-and-response riffs, and extended organ and sax solos. Raw analog live-band recording, slow-burning build over many bars.
