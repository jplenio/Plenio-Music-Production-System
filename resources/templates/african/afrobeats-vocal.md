Warm, sunny and irresistibly rhythmic modern Afrobeats: syncopated shaker and log-drum percussion, rolling melodic bass, bright brass stabs, clean highlife-style guitar licks and a relaxed, confident vocal hook with soft ad-libs. Laid-back swing behind the beat, spacious modern pop production. Lyrics short, repetitive and uplifting, about joy, movement and living in the moment.
