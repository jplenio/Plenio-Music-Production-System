Meditative Hindustani classical piece: tanpura drone, slow alap on sitar, gradual entry of tabla in a clear rhythmic cycle, soft bansuri flute answering the sitar. Slow melodic unfolding of one raga, microtonal ornaments, gradual acceleration toward an intense final section. Contemplative and spiritual.
