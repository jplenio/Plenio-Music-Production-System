Colorful, dramatic Bollywood playback song: lush orchestral strings, driving dhol and tabla rhythms, bright brass, dholak fills, harmonium and a passionate vocal duet with ornamented melismatic phrasing. Sweeping melodies, joyful dance interlude and an emotional cinematic arc. Lyrics about love, celebration and destiny.
