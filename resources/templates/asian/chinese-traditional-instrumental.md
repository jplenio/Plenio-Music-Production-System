Elegant and evocative Chinese traditional music: erhu lead, guzheng arpeggios, pipa tremolos and dizi bamboo flute over subtle ambient textures and soft frame drum. Pentatonic melodies, delicate ornaments and glissandi, spacious poetic atmosphere alternating between stillness and flowing motion.
