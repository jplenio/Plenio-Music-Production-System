Fast, raw and rebellious punk rock: three-chord power-chord riffs, driving eighth-note bass, hammering drums with a fast snare and an energetic shouted vocal with gang-vocal backups. Short punchy sections, no solos, no-frills live production, short and furious. Lyrics about frustration, freedom and fighting back.
