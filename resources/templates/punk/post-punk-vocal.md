Cold post-punk: driving melodic bass leading the song, angular chorus-drenched guitar stabs, motorik drums with heavy toms and a detached, half-spoken baritone vocal. Repetitive hypnotic structure, minor key, cavernous reverb and a stark, gray atmosphere. Lyrics fragmentary, about distance, cities and dread.
