Bright pop-punk: fast palm-muted power chords, octave lead riffs, galloping bass, driving drums with a skate-punk beat and a nasal earnest vocal with big 'whoa-oh' gang harmonies. Huge singalong chorus, a half-time bridge, polished but energetic production. Lyrics about growing up in a boring town, a summer and a broken heart.
