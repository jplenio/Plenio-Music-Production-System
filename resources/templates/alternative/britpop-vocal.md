Swaggering 1990s Britpop: jangly and slightly overdriven guitars, melodic bass, tight live drums, occasional Hammond organ and string accents, and a nasal charismatic lead vocal with big singalong backing harmonies. Bold anthemic chorus, classic verse-chorus-bridge structure, warm analog production. Lyrics about ordinary life, ambition and youthful defiance.
