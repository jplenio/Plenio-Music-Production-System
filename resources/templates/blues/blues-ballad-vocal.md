Aching slow blues ballad: open, clean guitar with reverb, Hammond organ pads, soft brushed drums, upright bass and a heartbroken, restrained vocal that grows into a raw belt. Long held notes, space between phrases, one soulful guitar solo. Lyrics about being left alone at the end of the night.
