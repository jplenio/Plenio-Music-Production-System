Analog synthesizer space music: slow-evolving pad layers, wide stereo reverb, filtered sawtooth lead with long portamento, gentle arpeggiated sequence, deep sub-bass drone, tape hiss and analog warmth. Dreamy and weightless, cosmic atmosphere. No drums. 1970s European electronic, cinematic and meditative.
