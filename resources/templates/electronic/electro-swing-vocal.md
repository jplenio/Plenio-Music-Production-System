Playful electro swing: sampled 1920s big-band brass and clarinet loops with crackle, over a modern four-on-the-floor beat, walking bass, ukulele-banjo chops and filtered breakdowns. Vintage-sounding crooning vocals with megaphone-style filtering. Fun, dancey and theatrical. Lyrics about a wild night out in an old speakeasy.
