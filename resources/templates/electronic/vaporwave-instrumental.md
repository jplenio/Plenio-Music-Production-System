Vaporwave: heavily slowed and pitched-down 1980s soft-rock or smooth-jazz sample, drowned in reverb and chorus, with tape wobble, wow-and-flutter, muffled drums and long looping sections. Nostalgic, hazy, slightly melancholic and deliberately lo-fi.
