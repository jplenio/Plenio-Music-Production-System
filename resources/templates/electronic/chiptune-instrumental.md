Upbeat 8-bit chiptune: square, pulse and triangle wave channels plus noise percussion, fast arpeggiated chords, a bouncy memorable melody and a driving bass line. Classic handheld-console sound with strict channel limits, looping A-B-A structure, joyful and nostalgic.
