Classic Broadway 'I want' ballad: solo piano intro, gradually adding strings, woodwinds and a full orchestral swell, with a powerful theatrical belt in the final chorus and a held last note. Clear dramatic build, rubato phrasing, one recurring hook line. Lyrics about longing for a different life and finally deciding to reach for it.
