Deep, spacious dub: rumbling bass leading everything, stripped-back one-drop drums, offbeat skank fragments drifting in and out through enormous tape echo and spring reverb, sudden drop-outs and filter sweeps. Meditative, psychedelic mixing-desk performance built on reggae's heartbeat.
