Right at the top, two cards. One click switches each, in the graph and in App mode.

@ HQ Mastering
HQ Mastering is off by default. Switch it on, and UniverSR regenerates the highs MiniMax leaves a little dull: clearly less AI shimmer, but it takes about twice as long.

@ Cover Art is on
Cover Art is on: Flux two Klein paints the cover from the artwork prompt. Off, and no image model loads.
