# Narration of party-songs.mjs (Tutorial 9 · Party songs). Format: lib/voice.mjs.

@ card 9 · Party songs
Songs made at the party - from the player, and from your guests' phones.

@ card Plenio at a glance
Plenio is a complete song production suite, right on your own PC. [pause:0.2s] Let it make a whole batch of finished songs on its own, [pause:0.2s] or take every word and every note into your own hands, DAW style. It's the perfect home for YuE2. [pause:0.3s] And it brings a player of its own, made for these songs, with a party mode where everyone makes songs from their own phone, straight into the playlist.

@ card In this video
In this video: songs made from the player. Presets and takes, guests making songs on their phones, and the party reacting.

@ The Plenio Player plays your songs on its own
The Plenio Player plays your songs on its own. To make new ones, it needs the studio: ComfyUI, on the same computer.

@ Settings, The studio
Settings, The studio. Right now, ComfyUI is not running.

@ Start the studio runs it
Start the studio runs it in the background. No window, and no workflow to open.

@ Or let it start by itself
Or let it start by itself, the moment a song is ordered...

@ … and stop after half an hour
...and stop after half an hour without a song. Then the graphics card is free again.

@ Party songs: let people make songs
Party songs: let people make songs. Logged-in users, and the guests on their phones.

@ You decide how many songs
You decide how many songs each person may have in the making, and how long they may be.

@ And whether the party sees who made a song
And whether the party sees who made a song, while it plays.

@ Only a guest’s song is a party song
Only a guest's song is a party song. Yours, and the users', are saved like songs made in ComfyUI.

@ Live Play runs the party
Live Play runs the party. Every song made from now on joins it: next, at the end, or anywhere.

@ Make a song: a new song
Make a song. A new song, or a cover of a recording you drop in.

@ Presets set the whole form at once
Presets set the whole form at once. Best quality: HQ mastering, cover art, and the best of three takes for the words...

@ … or Party, fast
...or Party, fast: about a minute and a half, with cover art, ready in a few minutes.

@ Your own settings keep a name
Your own settings keep a name of their own.

@ Pick a style
Pick a style: a vibe, any template by name, or Surprise me. It fills every field below.

@ Say what it is about
Say what it is about, in a few words. [pause:0.2s] Or write your own lyrics, sung as written.

@ How many: two takes of one song
How many: two takes of one song. The same words and notes, sung and played twice. Keep the one you like best.

@ Generate. The song is first in line
Generate. The song is first in line, and its second take follows when it's made.

@ ComfyUI was not running
ComfyUI was not running, so the player starts it now.

@ The studio is ready
The studio is ready, and the song goes to it.

@ Your songs: the line
Your songs: the line, what the studio does now, and roughly when your song will be ready.

@ The writer writes the title | optional
The writer model writes the title, the style and the lyrics.

@ Plenio composes the score | optional
Plenio composes the score.

@ YuE2 sings and plays it | optional
YuE2 sings and plays it.

@ The cover is painted | optional
The cover is painted.

@ Mastered, timed and saved | optional
Mastered, timed, and saved.

@ Made - with its title and its cover
Made, with its title and its cover.

@ It joined Live Play
It joined Live Play, marked new.

@ Guests in your network
Guests in your network: everyone on the party's Wi-Fi scans the QR code.

@ Reactions: as many per song | optional
Reactions: as many per song as they like, or one per song and guest. A tap on another one changes it.

@ On the phone: what plays
On the phone: what plays, what comes next, and Make your own song.

@ The same simple form
The same simple form: a style, a few words. The host decides how long a guest's song may be.

@ Their song is in line
Their song is in line, with a rough time it will be ready.

@ Everybody’s songs
Everybody's songs: the second take first, then the guest's song. You may move any song, or take it out.

@ Both are ready
Both are ready, and the guest's song plays next.

@ While it plays, the party sees
While it plays, the party sees whose song it is, and from which party. Large as it begins, at every new section, and as it ends.

@ And the guests react
And the guests react: one tap on their phone...

@ … and it floats up the big screen
...and it floats up the big screen. Live Play counts how the room liked each song.

@ Every song keeps its reactions | optional
Every song keeps its reactions. Its page shows them, apart from your favourite heart.

@ Any finished song can be made again | optional
Any finished song can be made again: a new take, a new song like it, or a cover of it.

@ Make songs with your guests
Make songs with your guests, and the player does the rest.
