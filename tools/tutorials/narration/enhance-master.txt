@ card 4 · Enhance

Plenio is a complete song production suite, right on your own PC. [pause:0.3s] And it brings a player of its own, made for these songs, with a party mode where everyone makes songs from their own phone, straight into the playlist.

and Plenio's folder under Extensions.

@ 4 · Enhance & Master finishes
Template four: Enhance and Master. [pause:0.3s] It finishes a recording you already have.

@ Four numbered sections
Four numbered sections. Every stage of the songs' Master block is here, as nodes you can reach.

@ SOURCE - the HQ Mastering card
At the top, the HQ Mastering card. Then the recording, and Stems under it, off until you want to rebalance a mix.

@ AUDIO HQ - before the EQ
Audio HQ comes first: stage one calms the shimmer, two regenerates the highs, three dries a short room, and is optional.

@ MASTER - an EQ for the tone
Master: an EQ shapes the tone, then Loudness and Dynamics sets the level.

@ FINISH - the preview
And Finish: a preview, and the export, with the tags, and the original kept safe.

@ At the very top, the HQ Mastering card
At the very top, the HQ Mastering card. One click switches it, in the graph and in App mode.

@ HQ Mastering is on here
Here it's on: UniverSR regenerates the highs. Clearly less AI shimmer, and the cure for a dull old MP3. It takes about twice as long.

@ card Master a take
Part one: master a take, with the default settings.

@ Load the recording:
First, load the recording into Source.

@ Our example: a YuE2 take
Our example is a YuE2 take, straight from the model, and not mastered yet.

@ Under it, Stems:
Under it sits Stems. Select it and press Control B to rebalance a finished mix: vocals, drums, bass and other. The mixer is inside the block.

@ Audio HQ: there is no brief here
There's no brief in this template, so the presets say what they are: standard, or gentle, strong or wide, for your kind of music.

@ HQ 2 runs because HQ Mastering is on
The second stage runs because HQ Mastering is on: the cure for a dull, band-limited file, like an old MP3.

@ HQ 3 is bypassed
The third stage is bypassed: it dries a short metallic room, but it also dulls the highs. Press Control B to try it.

@ EQ: “match preset”, Warm
The EQ measures the song, and proposes a few gentle, warm bands.

@ The curve under the node
After the run, this curve shows those bands, right over the song's own spectrum.

@ Loudness & Dynamics: streaming
Loudness aims at streaming: minus fourteen LUFS, true peaks at most minus one.

@ compression: Balanced
Compression adds gentle glue, or leaves it to the limiter.

@ Press Run.
Press Run.

@ HQ 1 calms the stereo shimmer | optional
The first stage calms the shimmer,

@ HQ 2: UniverSR generates | optional
UniverSR generates new highs,

@ EQ measures the song | optional
the EQ measures and matches,

@ Loudness & Dynamics brings it
Loudness and Dynamics brings up the level,

@ Export writes the files
and Export writes the files.

@ HQ 2’s report:
The second stage reports what it did: the profile, UniverSR from eight kilohertz, and how the highs changed.

@ The match: gentle bands
Here's the match: a few gentle bands, over the spectrum of the take. [pause:0.3s] Subtle, on purpose.

@ Measured, not estimated
Measured, not guessed: minus fourteen LUFS. Target reached.

@ Listen in the preview | +5
Let's listen to the master.

@ Export: FLAC and MP3
Export wrote a FLAC and an MP3 with the source's tags, the original, a release record, and subtitles of the words it heard. No sheet music: a recording has no score.

@ card Your own sound
Part two: make it your own sound.

@ “Edit these bands”
Edit these bands makes the proposal yours.

@ Drag a handle:
Drag a handle to change its frequency and gain. [pause:0.3s] The mouse wheel sets its width, and a double-click adds a new band.

@ The presets menu
Or pick a ready-made curve from the presets.

@ target: Apple Music
For Apple Music and podcasts, choose a slightly quieter target: minus sixteen LUFS.

@ Run again.
Run it again.

@ Loudness & Dynamics brings it
The new settings are applied,

@ Export writes the files
and Export writes the new files.

@ -16 LUFS, measured
Minus sixteen, measured. If a target needs too much limiting, the node stops short, and says so.

@ The new master | +4
Here's the new master, with the original right next to it for comparison.

@ card The lyrics, corrected
Part three: the lyrics of a sung recording, corrected before the release.

@ The lyrics: Export writes them
Export writes the lyrics as subtitles, into the lyrics tag and into the record. As the speech recognition hears them, misheard words included.

@ Select the two nodes of Lyrics
Select the two nodes of the Lyrics block, a click and a shift-click, and press control B. Now they take part.

@ The run stops at the Song Sheet
The run stops at the Song Sheet, and waits for your approval. Nothing is exported yet.

@ Open it: “Edit lyrics & text”
Open it.

@ Lyrics: the words as the ASR heard them
The lyrics tab shows the words as they were heard, section by section.

@ It heard | optional
Here it heard a few words differently than the song sings them. Type them as they are sung.

@ Every line was heard right | optional
This time every line was heard right. A word you change here is what Export writes.

@ Approve: these words go into the release
Approve. These words go into the release.

@ Run again: Export times your words
Run again. Export times your words to the singing.

@ Your words: in the subtitles
Your words are in the subtitles, in the lyrics tag and in the record. The sung subtitles keep what was heard.
