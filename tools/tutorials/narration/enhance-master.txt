# Narration of enhance-master.mjs (Tutorial 4 · Enhance & Master). Format: lib/voice.mjs.

@ card 4 · Enhance
A recording that needs a final polish? This template masters it, no graphics card needed.

@ Open the template browser
Open the template browser,

@ Plenio’s templates:
and Plenio's folder under Extensions.

@ 4 · Enhance & Master finishes
Template four: Enhance and Master. [pause:0.4s] It finishes a recording you already have.

@ Three steps, from left to right
It's just three steps, from left to right.

@ SOURCE - the recording
Source: a take you rendered earlier, or any song file.

@ MASTER - an EQ for the tone
Master: an EQ shapes the tone, then Loudness and Dynamics sets the level.

@ FINISH - the preview
And Finish: a preview, and the export, with tags, and the original kept safe.

@ Optional: Stems rebalances
Two optional blocks stay off: Stems, to rebalance a mix, and Refine, for dull files.

@ card Master a take
Part one: master a take, with the default settings.

@ Load the recording:
First, load the recording into Source.

@ Our example: a YuE2 take
Our example is a YuE2 take, straight from the model, and not mastered yet.

@ EQ: “match preset”, Warm
The EQ matches the tone: it measures the song, and proposes gentle, warm bands.

@ The curve under the node
After the run, the curve shows those bands, right over the song's own spectrum.

@ Loudness & Dynamics: streaming
Loudness aims at streaming level: minus fourteen LUFS, peaks below zero.

@ compression: Balanced
Compression: gentle glue. Or just the limiter.

@ Press Run.
Press Run.

@ EQ measures the song
The EQ measures and matches,

@ Loudness & Dynamics brings it
Loudness and Dynamics brings up the level,

@ Export writes the files
and Export writes the files.

@ The match: gentle bands
Here's the match: a few gentle bands, over the spectrum of the take. [pause:0.4s] Subtle, on purpose.

@ Measured, not estimated
And these numbers are measured: minus fourteen LUFS. Target reached.

@ Listen in the preview | +6
Let's listen to the master.

@ Export: FLAC and MP3
Export wrote a FLAC and an MP3 with the source's tags, plus the original and a release record.

@ card Your own sound
Part two: make it your own sound.

@ “Edit these bands”
Edit these bands: now the bands are yours.

@ Drag a handle:
Drag a handle to change its frequency and gain. [pause:0.3s] The mouse wheel sets its width, and a double-click adds a new band.

@ The presets menu
Or pick a ready-made curve from the presets.

@ target: Apple Music
For Apple Music and podcasts, pick a slightly quieter target: minus sixteen LUFS.

@ Run again.
Run it again.

@ Loudness & Dynamics brings it
The new settings are applied,

@ Export writes the files
and exported.

@ -16 LUFS, measured
Minus sixteen, measured. And if a target needs too much limiting, the node stops short, and tells you.

@ The new master | +6
Here's the new master, with the original right next to it.
