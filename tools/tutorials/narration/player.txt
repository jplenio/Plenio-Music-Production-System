# Narration of player.mjs (Tutorial 8 · Plenio Player). Format: lib/voice.mjs.

@ card 8 · Plenio Player
Your songs deserve a proper stage. This is the Plenio Player.

@ card Plenio at a glance
Plenio is a complete song production suite, right on your own PC. [pause:0.2s] Let it make a whole batch of finished songs on its own, [pause:0.2s] or take every word and every note into your own hands, DAW style. It's the perfect home for YuE2. [pause:0.3s] And it brings a player of its own, made for these songs, with a party mode where everyone makes songs from their own phone, straight into the playlist.

@ card In this video
In this video: the Plenio Player. Your songs, their pages, the visuals, the lyrics, playlists, Live Play, videos and sharing.

@ The Plenio Player plays the songs
The Plenio Player plays the songs you made with Plenio, right in your browser.

@ In ComfyUI it is one click away
In ComfyUI, it's one click away: the Plenio Player button, right next to Run.

@ Export Release has a button too
Export Release has a button too. It opens the song you just exported.

@ If the player is not running yet
If the player isn't running yet, ComfyUI starts it, and opens it in a tab of its own.

@ Who is listening?
Who is listening? The player asks once, on every device.

@ A user keeps the same playlists | optional
A user keeps the same playlists, likes and settings on every device. The admin sets them up.

@ The admin logs in only
The admin logs in only on the player's own computer, with the player's playlists and settings.

@ A guest’s playlists and likes
And a guest's playlists and likes stay in that browser.

@ The player is a program of its own
The player is a program of its own, so it keeps running when you close ComfyUI. And without ComfyUI, just double-click Plenio Player dot bat.

@ It finds every song Export Release wrote
It finds every song Export Release wrote into ComfyUI's output folder.

@ Search looks through titles
Search looks through titles, lyrics, genres, moods and briefs.

@ Fields narrow it down
Fields narrow it down: genre, tempo, key, model, language, or just your favourites.

@ The chips filter
The chips filter by your most common genres, moods, models and creative modes.

@ A grid or a list
A grid or a list, sorted by date, title, length, tempo or loudness.

@ Sorted by title, the letters at the side
Sorted by title, the letters at the side jump anywhere in the list. Sorted by date, they're the months.

@ Press play on a cover
Press play on a cover. A click on the card itself opens the song's page.

@ The bar shows the song’s section
The bar always shows the section, and the line that's sung right now.

@ The waveform is the seek bar
The waveform is the seek bar. It marks the sections, and under the mouse, it shows the line sung there.

@ The bright square on the right
The bright square on the right moves with the music. Click it, and Now playing opens.

@ ⓘ beside the song opens its page
The little i beside the song opens its page, too.

@ Its stars: give your own | optional
Its stars: give your own, by ear. Until you do, the measured ones stand there, faded.

@ Four of five: your stars | optional
Four out of five: your stars, kept just like your playlists.

@ Its lyrics light up
Its lyrics light up while they are sung. The times were measured by listening, when the song was exported.

@ A click on a line plays | optional
Click a line, and the song plays from there.

@ The notation follows the music | optional
The notation follows the music. The notes that sound light up, and a click on a note jumps right there.

@ The details: every field of the brief
The details list every field of the brief. One you left empty shows its default. Then the model, the writer, the loudness, and the seeds, to make the same take again.

@ How it was made | optional
How it was made: when. And for a song made since one point four, how long it took, on what, and with which lyrics fidelity.

@ How it came out, measured | optional
How it came out, measured: the lyrics sung as written, the harmony, the beat, and what went wrong. A hint where to listen first.

@ Every file to download
And every file to download: the audio, the sheet music, the subtitles, the cover, and the record.

@ The song’s other takes | optional
The song's other takes, to compare them,

@ … and songs alike | optional
and songs that are alike.

@ Back to your songs
Back to your songs, the page shows the one that plays, even among hundreds of songs.

@ F opens Now playing
Press F for Now playing. The stage: the cover, and the lyrics as they're sung.

@ The sections are chips | optional
The sections are chips. One click, and the chorus plays.

@ Lyrics: large
Lyrics view: big, and painted syllable by syllable, like karaoke.

@ Notation: the sheet scrolls along
Notation view: the sheet scrolls along with the music, and the lyrics sit at the side.

@ And the visual, full screen
And the visual, full screen, with the lyrics as subtitles. Leave the mouse alone, and the controls fade away.

@ The visuals’ settings
These are the visual settings.

@ Match the music picks
Match the music picks a visual for the song's genre, mood and tempo.

@ Random picks from the visuals you tick
Random picks from the visuals you tick. Cycle goes through them in turn, and Fixed keeps just one.

@ Tick the ones you like
Tick the ones you like. There are twenty-five classics, fifty-one Plenio Drop visuals, and Plenio Nova's twenty-two scenes, in folded groups, with a search.

@ Find one by name | optional
Find one by name, or by what it shows. Or pick a mood.

@ They change with every song
They can change with every song, at every section, every few seconds, or when the music drops in.

@ A song can have a visual of its own
A song can have a visual of its own, and so can a playlist. The song's choice wins, then the playlist's, then the mode.

@ Plenio Nova: scenes
Plenio Nova: scenes the graphics card works out for every pixel. A galaxy, northern lights, the Mandelbulb, a black hole that bends the starlight.

@ The player offers only the visuals this computer draws smoothly - here
The player offers only the visuals this computer draws smoothly. Here, the heaviest ones are left out.

@ The player offers only the visuals | optional
The player offers only the visuals this computer draws smoothly.

@ Plenio Drop: visuals like
Plenio Drop: visuals like Winamp's MilkDrop, made anew and drawn on the graphics card. And one is made up fresh, every time.

@ Write your own
Want your own? Edit a copy saves the one that plays as a short file of numbers and formulas. Save a change, and the visual changes right away.

@ How the lyrics look
And how the lyrics look is up to you: a font, a colour, and an effect, like neon, glow, bounce, typewriter, or on the beat. In the player, and in videos.

@ N shows the next one
Press N for the next one.

@ In a song’s last seconds, Up next
In a song's last seconds, Up next names the one that follows. Made for the big screen at a party.

@ A music model does not always sing | optional
A music model doesn't always sing what it was given. It repeats a line, makes one up, or leaves one out.

@ So the player has both | optional
So the player has both: the lyrics as written, and as sung. That's what the take really sings, heard when the song was exported.

@ Auto: a take that departs | optional
On Auto, a take that strays far from its lyrics shows them as sung - as the take's vocals were heard.

@ One click switches | optional
One click switches, and the song keeps your choice.

@ W does it too | optional
W does it too, or the chip in the bar. The notation always follows the written lyrics.

@ Full lyrics: the whole text | optional
Full lyrics: the whole text, with its sections. Copy it for a new song, with or without the section tags, and the style too.

@ The song’s page: the lyrics | optional
The song's page: the lyrics, the prompts, every setting and the files, on one page. Export Release writes it next to every song.

@ A new playlist: plus, a name
A new playlist: click plus, and give it a name.

@ Add songs with the search
Add songs with the search, and drag them into order.

@ A playlist can have its own colour
Every playlist can have its own colour, and its own visual.

@ Next to yours: favourites
Next to your own playlists, there are smart ones: favourites, most played, recently played, and never played.

@ The queue: what comes next
The queue shows what comes next. Reorder it, or save it as a playlist.

@ Crossfades between songs
Songs crossfade into each other, and the sleep timer fades the music out.

@ A right-click on a song
Right-click a song to play it next, add it to a playlist, start a radio of songs that are alike, or remove it.

@ Live Play: a playlist for a party
Live Play is a playlist for a party, and you change it while it plays.

@ Make songs in ComfyUI meanwhile
Make songs in ComfyUI meanwhile. As soon as Export Release has written one, it joins: next, at the end, or anywhere.

@ There it is - marked new
There it is. Marked new, and playing next.

@ Guests in your network: switch it on
Guests in your network: switch it on, and everyone on the same Wi-Fi opens the live playlist on their phone. There's a QR code, and an address with a random part.

@ Show it big, for a screen
Show it big, for a screen the whole room can see. And a new address, at any time, locks the old one out.

@ On the phone: the song that plays
On the phone: the song that plays, and what comes next.

@ … and if you allow it, guests move songs
And if you allow it, guests move songs, take them out, and add their favourites. The music keeps playing on your computer.

@ And they react to the song that plays | optional
And they react to the song that plays...

@ … it floats up the big screen | optional
...it floats up the big screen, and every song keeps its reactions, apart from your hearts.

@ The playlist follows
The playlist follows, and tells you what the guests did.

@ A song as a video
A song as a video: its visual, the lyrics as karaoke, and the notes following the music. In Full HD, in 4K, or upright for Shorts.

@ Seven steps of quality | optional
Seven steps of quality, from low to best. Or a bitrate of your own.

@ Made on the graphics card
It's made on the graphics card, faster than the song plays. An MP4, in the player's videos folder.

@ Share your songs as a player
Share your songs as a player: one HTML file, with the songs as MP3. Send it, and anyone just double-clicks it.

@ It plays by itself in any browser | optional
It plays by itself, in any browser: the lyrics, the notation, the visuals. Nothing to install.

@ A/B: the unmastered take | optional
A, B: the unmastered take, at exactly the same moment, without a gap.

@ Speed, from three quarters
Speed goes from three quarters to one and a quarter, and the pitch stays the same.

@ Every key is listed
Every keyboard shortcut is listed under the question mark.

@ Help and tutorials: short guides
Help and tutorials: short guides by topic, and every tutorial as a video. Press H, anywhere.

@ A click plays a tutorial right here
A click plays a tutorial right here. Nothing is loaded from YouTube before you click.

@ The search finds the lines
The search finds the lines of every guide that answer it.

@ Settings starts with the version
Settings starts with the version of the player that's running.

@ Its sections are chips
Its sections are chips: one click jumps right there.

@ The studio: when ComfyUI does not run | optional
The studio: when ComfyUI isn't running, the player starts it for you, the way you last started it, with your models folder. Tutorial nine shows more.

@ Accounts: who logged in | optional
Accounts: who's logged in, and the users the admin has set up.

@ A name, a password and a group | optional
A name, a password, and a group: listens only, makes songs, or helps as an admin. A user logs in on any device that opens the player.

@ Everything you set is kept
Everything you set is kept on this computer, by the player itself, not by the browser.

@ A backup to download
There's a backup to download, and to restore, here or on another computer.

@ Choose the folders it reads | optional
Choose the folders it reads. And the other music in them plays too, with its tags and lyrics.

@ Sound: the MP3 | optional
Under Sound: play the MP3 where a song has one. Less data, for a phone in the network.

@ Lyrics: the rule for every song | optional
And for the lyrics, the rule for every song: auto, as written, or as sung.

@ Started from ComfyUI, the player has no window
Started from ComfyUI, the player has no window. Stop the player ends it.

@ And Open in ComfyUI takes the song back
One more click sends the song back to ComfyUI.

@ Its workflow opens
Its workflow opens, with the brief, the lyrics, the score and the settings, ready for another take.

@ Make songs in ComfyUI
Make your songs in ComfyUI, and listen to them in the player.

@ card Plenio Player
That's the Plenio Player. Enjoy your music!
