# Narration of yue2-cover.mjs (Tutorial 2 · YuE2 · Cover). Format: lib/voice.mjs.

@ card 2 · YuE2 · Cover
Welcome back! This time, we turn an existing recording into a brand-new cover.

@ Open the template browser
Open the template browser once more.

@ Plenio’s templates:
Plenio's templates are under Extensions.

@ 2 · YuE2 · Cover makes
We pick template two: YuE2 Cover. [pause:0.4s] It makes a new version of a recording, one you own, or are allowed to use.

@ The template reads from left to right
Again, the template reads from left to right.

@ SOURCE - the recording
Source: the recording to cover.

@ COVER - the Cover Brief
The Cover Brief sets the new style, and what happens to the vocals.

@ SCORE - SheetSage2
SheetSage2 transcribes the music into a score. Arrange can re-arrange it, and the Score sheet shows it.

@ LYRICS - the original words
Lyrics: the original words, brand-new lyrics, or just section tags.

@ TEXT - Song Sheet · Text
The Text sheet shows the words and the style YuE2 gets.

@ RENDER - YuE2 renders the cover
YuE2 renders the cover, and Check Vocals picks the best take.

@ FINISH - Master
And Master and Export finish the job.

@ card A new cover every run
Part one: a new cover, every run. Straight through, no stops.

@ Load the recording:
First, load the recording, right here in Source recording.

@ Our example:
Our example is a four-minute synth-pop song.

@ Longer songs:
For longer songs, switch on Excerpt, and cover one part at a time.

@ The Cover Brief says
The Cover Brief describes the new version.

@ mode: “new cover every run”
With new cover every run, each run makes a different version, without any stops.

@ genre and mood:
Genre and mood set the new style.

@ vocals: “instrumental”
Instrumental: an instrument plays the melody.

@ harmony: keep the original chords
Keep the original chords, or let YuE2 re-harmonize.

@ arrangement: “simple” keeps the transcription
The arrangement stays on simple, which keeps the transcription as it is. [pause:0.3s] The experimental creative modes, with their sliders, get a video of their own: tutorial seven.

@ Press Run.
Now, press Run.

@ SheetSage2 transcribes the recording
SheetSage2 transcribes the melody, the chords and the sections,

@ YuE2 renders the cover from the score
YuE2 renders the cover from that score,

@ Check Vocals listens
Check Vocals listens to the take,

@ Master and Export finish the cover
and Master and Export finish it.

@ Done: the instrumental cover | +7 | seed 8
Done! Here's our instrumental cover. [pause:0.5s] Let's have a listen.

@ Press Run again for another version
Run again for another version. The transcription is reused, so only the new cover renders.

@ card One cover, stop to review
Part two: one cover, with stops to review. You check the score and the words first.

@ mode: “one cover, stop to review”
Switch the mode to one cover, stop to review. The run now pauses at both Song Sheets.

@ vocals: “original lyrics”
And set vocals to original lyrics: YuE2 sings the words of the recording.

@ voice: describe the singer
Describe the singer you'd like to hear.

@ Press Run. The transcription
Press Run. [pause:0.4s] The transcription from part one is simply reused.

@ Stop 1: Song Sheet · Score
Stop number one: the Score sheet waits for your approval.

@ Open it with
Open it with Edit Song Sheet,

@ ⛶ gives the editor
and give the editor the whole window.

@ This sheet holds the score
This is the score SheetSage2 transcribed. The words come at the next stop, and they follow this score.

@ First a quick tour, then the details
First, a quick tour, then the details. The score editor shows one score in several views, and they always stay in step.

@ On top, the piano roll
On top, the piano roll: the notes of the melody and of the second voice, bar by bar. The bar numbers are the ruler.

@ Above the notes, the chord lane
Above the notes, the chord lane, with each chord where it starts.

@ For a cover, the original recording runs in a lane | optional
For a cover, the original recording runs in a lane of its own. [pause:0.3s] And the pink curve is the pitch the singer really sang.

@ The roll’s toolbar
The roll's toolbar: draw or select, the voice you draw into, the grid, copy and paste, zoom, and quantize.

@ Under the roll, the transport
Under the roll, the transport: play from the cursor, record with a MIDI keyboard, the sounds of the tracks, [pause:0.2s] and what you hear: the notes, the original, or both.

@ Below, the notation
Below, the notation: both voices, with the chords, the sections, and the lyrics. Click a note there, and it's selected everywhere.

@ On the right, the inspector
On the right, the inspector, for the exact values of a note, a chord, or a bar.

@ And the song’s sections with the bar strip
And the song's sections, with the bar strip, to arrange the song, like on a DAW's arranger track.

@ At the top: undo and the edit buttons
At the top: undo and the edit buttons, the layouts, and the files: MIDI, MusicXML, the notation as PDF or picture, and the project.

@ “keys” lists every key
And keys lists every shortcut and mouse gesture.

@ Shift + mouse wheel
Shift and the mouse wheel scroll to where the singing starts.

@ Click a note: | seed 6
Editing works just like in the song video: click a note to select it everywhere,

@ The arrow keys move it
move it with the arrow keys, and undo with Control Z.

@ The toolbar does the same
The toolbar offers the same moves.

@ Drag a note to move it
Drag a note to move it, [pause:0.3s] and undo again.

@ Drag its right end
Drag its right edge to change its length.

@ Draw mode:
Draw a new note on an empty spot,

@ Select mode:
or frame several notes in Select mode,

@ … and move them together
and move them together.

@ Ctrl+Z again
One more undo, and the melody is back.

@ Click in the ruler - the bar numbers
Now, the cursor. Click into the ruler to set it, just like in Cubase.

@ Playback starts at the cursor
Playback starts at the cursor, with a green line following along. [pause:0.4s] Stop, and the cursor stays put.

@ The transport sits right under the roll
The transport sits right under the roll, within reach. Space plays and stops, Home and End jump to either end.

@ The original recording plays right under the notes
And here's the clever part for covers: the original recording plays right under the notes, bar by bar. [pause:0.3s] And A-B switches between them, at once.

@ The pink curve is the sung pitch | optional
The pink curve is the singer's actual pitch, taken from the original vocals. [pause:0.3s] Where it leaves a note, the transcription is off: a wrong pitch, or a missed note.

@ A cover far from the original? | optional
A cover far from the original? Untick wave and sung, and the roll shows only your notes.

@ ⇆ align moves the recording | optional
And if the beats don't line up, align moves the recording against the bars.

@ Every track plays in a sound of its own
Every track plays in a sound of its own. Sounds, in the transport, sets them.

@ Vocal, Instrument, Chords and Guide
Vocal, instrument, chords, and guide: piano, strings, pad, organ, a sung ah, and more. [pause:0.2s] The play button lets you hear a few notes in each sound.

@ Presets set them for a kind of song
Presets set them all at once. Here: cover, check the transcription, with the original under the notes.

@ “save current as preset…” keeps your own
And save current as preset keeps your own: in ComfyUI, ready for every song, in every browser.

@ Arrange the song
Let's arrange. Select the chorus, and press Control D: a copy right after it.

@ Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓ move the selection
Control and the arrow keys move sections. We keep the copy, and undo the move.

@ With the original lyrics, the words follow
With the original lyrics, the words follow their bars. So the copied chorus will sing the same words again.

@ Export MusicXML hands the sheet music
Export MusicXML hands the sheet music, lyrics included, to MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico, or Cubase.

@ Export notation… gives the sheet music
Export notation gives the sheet music as a PDF, on A4 or letter pages, as a PNG or SVG picture, [pause:0.2s] or prints it.

@ A click on PDF
One click on PDF: both voices, the chords, the sections, and the lyrics, with the song's title, ready to print.

@ Save project keeps
And save project keeps the score, the guide notes, the lyrics, and the editor's settings together, in one file.

@ Approve: this score
Approve. The words will be placed into this score, extra chorus and all.

@ The node says ✓ approved at once
The node turns green at once. [pause:0.5s] Run again: now the words are transcribed, and the run stops at the Text sheet.

@ faster-whisper transcribes | optional
Faster Whisper listens to the vocals, and places every word into the score's sections.

@ Stop 2: Song Sheet · Text

@ Open the text. | seed 6

The Score tab shows the transcribed words, right over the notes they're sung on.

@ Shift + mouse wheel
Let's scroll to the vocals.

@ Each line over its phrase
Each line sits over its phrase. And to fix a word, just double-click its line.

@ The score can still be changed here
You can still change the score right here. Apply writes it back to the Score sheet.

@ Lyrics: the transcribed words
The Lyrics tab lists the words, section by section. Unsure words are highlighted, so you know where to look.

@ Style: the new style
Style: the new style, with the voice from the brief.

@ Title & artwork
And the title, with a prompt for optional cover art.

@ Approve: exactly these words
Approve: exactly these words go to YuE2.

@ ✓ approved at once
Approved. [pause:0.4s] One last run, and YuE2 renders the cover.

and Master and Export finish it.
