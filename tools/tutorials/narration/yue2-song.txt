@ card Plenio at a glance
Plenio is a complete song production suite, right on your own PC. [pause:0.2s] Let it make a whole batch of finished songs on its own, [pause:0.2s] or take every word and every note into your own hands, DAW style. It's the perfect home for YuE2. [pause:0.3s] And it brings a player of its own, made for these songs, with a party mode where everyone makes songs from their own phone, straight into the playlist.

In this video: the template and its Song Brief, a song in one run, a song with two review stops, and the score editor.

@ 1 · YuE2 · Song writes
We start with template one: YuE2 Song. [pause:0.3s] It writes a brand-new song, and renders it with the YuE2 music model.

@ The template reads from left to right
It reads from left to right, in numbered sections, side by side, one through six.

@ SONG - at the top two switch cards
Section one: two switch cards and the writer model at the top, then the Song Brief, what the song should be about.

@ WRITE - the writer drafts
Then the writer drafts the style and the lyrics, [pause:0.2s] and ten titles. Plenio picks one that none of your songs has yet.

@ TEXT - Song Sheet · Text, in teal
The Text sheet, in teal, shows exactly what YuE2 is going to sing. This is where you edit the words.

@ SCORE - YuE2 plans
Next, YuE2 plans the melody as a score, and Arrange can re-arrange it. The Score sheet, in violet, is where you edit the score.

@ RENDER - YuE2 renders
Here, YuE2 renders the actual song, and Best Take keeps the take that sings its lyrics best.

@ FINISH - Master brings
And finally, Master sets the loudness, and Export writes the files.

@ Above the render, COVER ART
Above the render sits Cover Art: it paints the song's cover, with a seed of its own.

@ At the very top, two cards
Right at the top, two cards. One click switches each, in the graph and in App mode.

@ HQ Mastering, off by default
HQ Mastering is off by default. Switch it on, and UniverSR regenerates the highs: clearly less AI shimmer, but the song takes about twice as long.

@ Cover Art is on
Cover Art is on: Flux two Klein paints the cover from the artwork prompt. Off, and no image model loads.

Everything starts in the Song Brief.

@ template: a starting point

Change any field you like. Each list holds the values of all two hundred and fifty templates, [pause:0.2s] and type your own takes anything else.

@ creativity: 100
Creativity: one hundred is the default, bold and dependable. Up to two hundred dares more: more surprises, and more misses.

@ lyrics fidelity: standard
Lyrics fidelity. Standard renders one take. High renders a second one, and keeps the take that sings more of the lyrics as written. Highest adds a third, when neither sang ninety-five percent. It takes longer. We take high.

@ Under the cards, the Writer model
Under the cards sits the writer model: one model for every writing step. Gemma 4 by default, or a local LLM from your machine.

@ It writes for every block that writes
It writes for every block that needs words: the lyrics and the style, the ten titles, and the arrangement.

@ In this mode every seed is amber
In this mode every seed is amber: each run draws new numbers, so every run writes new lyrics and renders a new take.

@ The number shown is always
The number you see is always the one that made what you hear. Like it? One click on the switch, and it stays: blue, same seed.

@ The Plan seed under YuE2
The Plan seed does the same for YuE2's melody and form.

@ Plenio · Master finishes every take
The Master block finishes every take: stems, Audio HQ, a gentle tone match, and the loudness.

@ stems is off
Stems is off. On, the song is split into vocals, drums, bass and other, and mixed your way.

@ The mixer is inside the block
The mixer lives inside the block. Open it with the icon at the top right.

@ First the stems
First the stems: a strip per stem, with fader, mute and solo, compression, reverb and delay. A neutral mix changes nothing.

@ Then Audio HQ
Then Audio HQ: stage one calms the stereo shimmer, stage two regenerates the highs when HQ Mastering is on.

@ And the master: a gentle tone match
And the master itself: a gentle tone match, then minus fourteen LUFS, with a true-peak limiter.

On top, the piano roll: the melody and the second voice, bar by bar.

@ Below, the notation
Further down, the notation, with chords, sections and lyrics. Click a note there, and it's selected everywhere.

On the right, the inspector, for the exact values of a note, a chord or a bar.

@ And the song’s sections with the bar strip
And the sections with the bar strip, like an arranger track.

At the top: undo, editing, the layouts, and the files: MIDI, MusicXML, PDF and the project.

Every track plays in a sound of its own, set under Sounds, in the transport.
