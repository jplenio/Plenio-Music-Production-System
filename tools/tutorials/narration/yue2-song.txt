@ card The score editor
Part three: the score editor. First a quick tour of everything it holds, then the details.

@ First a quick tour, then the details
First, a quick tour, then the details. The score editor shows one score in several views, and they always stay in step.

@ On top, the piano roll
On top, the piano roll: the notes of the melody and of the second voice, bar by bar. The bar numbers are the ruler.

@ Above the notes, the chord lane
Above the notes, the chord lane, with each chord where it starts.

@ Shift + mouse wheel | optional
Shift plus the mouse wheel scrolls through the bars.

@ Under it, the lyrics lane | optional
Below it, the lyrics lane: each line over its phrase, each syllable over its note.

@ The roll’s toolbar
The roll's toolbar: draw or select, the voice you draw into, the grid, copy and paste, zoom, and quantize.

@ Under the roll, the transport
Under the roll, the transport: play from the cursor, record with a MIDI keyboard, step input, the sounds of the tracks, loop, metronome, and speed.

@ Below, the notation
Below, the notation: both voices, with the chords, the sections, and the lyrics. Click a note there, and it's selected everywhere.

@ On the right, the inspector
On the right, the inspector, for the exact values of a note, a chord, or a bar.

@ And the song’s sections with the bar strip
And the song's sections, with the bar strip, to arrange the song, like on a DAW's arranger track.

@ At the top: undo and the edit buttons
At the top: undo and the edit buttons, the layouts, and the files: MIDI, MusicXML, the notation as PDF or picture, and the project.

@ “keys” lists every key
And keys lists every shortcut and mouse gesture.

@ The sheet warns

@ Fix it in the section list
That's easy to fix. In the section list, rename interlude to chorus.

@ Fixed: the warning is gone
Fixed! The warning is gone, and every word lands in the right section.

@ The notation shows the same syllables
The sheet music shows the same syllables, under the notes.

@ Double-click a line in the lyrics lane
To change a line, double-click it in the lyrics lane, and type, right where it's sung.

@ The words move onto their notes
The new words move onto their notes, and Apply will write them into the Text sheet.

@ Ctrl+Z undoes a lyrics edit
Control Z takes it back.

@ Click a note:
Now the notes. Click one, and all three views select it.

@ The arrow keys move it
The arrow keys move it: up and down in semitones, left and right along the grid.

@ The toolbar does the same
The toolbar can do the same, and more.

@ Drag a note to move it
Drag a note to move it in time and pitch, [pause:0.3s] and Control Z undoes any step.

@ Drag its right end

@ Draw mode:

@ Click in the ruler - the bar numbers
Next, the cursor. Click into the ruler, on the bar numbers, to place it, just like in Cubase.

@ Playback starts at the cursor
Playback starts right at the cursor. A green line follows the music, [pause:0.4s] and when you stop, the cursor stays where it was.

@ The transport sits right under the roll
The transport sits right under the roll, within reach. Space plays and stops, Home and End jump to either end.

@ Every track plays in a sound of its own
Every track plays in a sound of its own. Sounds, in the transport, sets them.
