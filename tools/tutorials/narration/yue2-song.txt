@ SCORE - YuE2 plans
Then YuE2 plans the melody as a score, and the Score sheet shows it.

@ RENDER - YuE2 renders
Here, YuE2 renders the actual song.

@ FINISH - Master
Finally, Master sets the loudness, and Export writes the files.

@ card The score editor
Part three: the score editor. A lot is new here, in version zero point four.

@ The score is shown three ways
The score is shown three ways: the piano roll on top, the sheet music below, and the inspector on the right.

@ On the left, the song’s sections
On the left: the sections, which you can now arrange, just like in a DAW.

@ Shift + mouse wheel
Shift plus the mouse wheel scrolls through the bars.

@ Also new: the lyrics stand over
Also new: the lyrics sit right above their notes, one syllable per note.

@ The sheet warns
But the sheet warns: the lyrics expect a chorus, not an interlude.

@ Fix it in the section list
That's easy to fix. In the section list, rename interlude to chorus.

@ Fixed: the warning is gone
Fixed! The warning is gone, and every word lands in the right section.

@ The notation shows the same syllables
The sheet music shows the same syllables, under the notes.

@ Double-click a line in the lyrics lane
To change a line, double-click it in the lyrics lane, and type, right where it's sung.

@ The words move onto their notes
The new words move onto their notes, and Apply will write them into the Text sheet.

@ Ctrl+Z undoes a lyrics edit
Control Z takes it back.

@ Click a note:
Now the notes. Click one, and all three views select it.

@ The arrow keys move it
The arrow keys move it: up and down in semitones, left and right along the grid.

@ The toolbar does the same
The toolbar can do the same, and more.

@ Drag a note to move it
Drag a note to move it in time and pitch, [pause:0.3s] and Control Z undoes any step.

@ Drag its right end
Drag its right edge to make it longer, or shorter.

@ Draw mode:
In Draw mode, drag across an empty spot, to draw a brand-new note.

@ Select mode:
Select mode is for groups: pull a frame around several notes,

@ … and move them together
and move them all at once. Here, two semitones up.

@ Ctrl+Z again
Control Z again, and the melody is back as it was.

@ Double-click a chord symbol
Chords work the same way. Double-click a chord symbol to change it, or the empty lane to add one.

@ New: click in the ruler
Next, the cursor. Click into the ruler, on the bar numbers, to place it, just like in Cubase.

@ Playback starts at the cursor
Playback starts right at the cursor. A green line follows the music, [pause:0.4s] and when you stop, the cursor stays where it was.

@ The transport sits right under the roll
The transport sits right under the roll, within reach. Space plays and stops, Home and End jump to either end.

@ Copy and paste work like
Copy and paste work just like in a DAW's key editor. Frame a phrase in Select mode,

@ … Ctrl+C copies it
press Control C, then set the cursor further along,

@ … and Ctrl+V pastes it there
and Control V pastes it there, replacing what was played.

@ Ctrl+Shift+V inserts it instead
Control Shift V inserts it instead, and everything after it moves back.

@ Ctrl+Z - the song is as it was
Undo, and the song is as it was. Cut and duplicate are here too, in the toolbar.

@ Arrange the song
Now, arranging. Select a section, here the chorus, and press Control D to duplicate it: notes, chords and all.

@ The lyrics follow
Watch the lyrics: the copy gets its words, too. And since YuE2 plans from them, this score is now kept as yours.

@ Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓ move the selection
Control and the arrow keys move sections around. We keep the copy, but undo the move.

@ New: the bar strip under the sections
New: the bar strip, right under the sections, arranges single bars, just like sections.

@ Click a bar, Shift+click the next one
Click a bar, and Shift click the next one,

@ … and the buttons above the strip
and the buttons above the strip work on both. Control D duplicates them, right after the originals.

@ Ctrl+← and Ctrl+→ move them
Control and the arrow keys move them, Delete removes them, and you can drag them too. With Alt, as a copy.

@ Ctrl+Z twice
Two undos, and the song is as it was. The guide track and the lyrics follow every bar edit.

@ Export MusicXML hands the sheet music
Export MusicXML hands the sheet music, lyrics included, to MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico, or Cubase.

@ Save project keeps
And Save project keeps the score, your guide notes and the lyrics together, in one file.

@ Approve: this score
Approve. This score, with the extra chorus, is what YuE2 will render.
